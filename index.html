Frequently Asked Questions

Everything university students and parents want to know about our curriculum, schedule, and career outcomes.

How do I manage this program alongside my regular university classes and exams?

Intelligement is built exclusively for enrolled university students. All interactive lecture workshops, code reviews, and mentor check-ins are conducted on weekends (Saturdays & Sundays). Furthermore, sprint deadlines are automatically suspended during university midterms and finals weeks so your academic GPA is never compromised.

Do I need advanced coding or prior fullstack experience to apply?

No advanced background is required. We look for curiosity, problem-solving mindset, and consistent commitment. Our curriculum begins with strong structural foundations in web architecture and progressively accelerates into enterprise backend logic (.NET & SQL Server) and modern AI automation.

What makes Intelligement different from typical short bootcamps or online courses?

Most bootcamps assign generic demo projects (like todo lists or weather apps) that impress no employers. At Intelligement, you build and ship real commercial MVPs (such as CAREHO, MobileXP, GoRent) with active users, enterprise databases, and AI tooling. You graduate with verified production software on your resume.

Is the program conducted in-person, online, or hybrid?

We utilize a flexible, high-touch hybrid format. Weekend labs and strategic architecture sessions are held at our designated campus lab in Islamabad (with Air University collaboration), complemented by high-definition interactive streaming and structured remote sprint standups for out-of-city students.

What certificates and credentials will I receive upon completion?

Graduates receive an official Intelligement Certification, a verified Internship Experience Letter specifying their technical contributions to live client MVPs, a permanent profile on our Wall of Glory, and personalized letters of recommendation for employers or international graduate school applications.

Who will be mentoring and reviewing my code during the training?

You receive direct mentorship from Mr. Asim Ali Fayyaz (PhD Scholar, Lecturer at Air University, Founder of Intelligement) alongside senior software architects, engineering managers, and Microsoft MVPs who actively guide sprint reviews and technical interviews.

What technologies and AI frameworks are integrated into the curriculum?

The curriculum spans Modern HTML5/CSS3, Advanced JavaScript/TypeScript, ASP.NET Core Web APIs, Entity Framework Core, SQL Server, Git/GitHub CI/CD, and practical AI workflow automation including OpenAI APIs, LangChain, and Agentic Automation pipelines.

How does Intelligement support student job placement and career advancement?

Through our partner brand network (including Futurenostics, Colinca, Softbuild), top cohort performers are directly recommended for fullstack engineering roles and paid software internships. We also conduct mock interviews, resume refinement, and technical portfolio reviews.
